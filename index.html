HOW TO PLAY

The round

You're dealt six cards and place four into a path. The path resolves left to right — you fight monsters, buy gear, hire allies, visit places — and whoever comes out the far end duels a ghost: a snapshot of another character from the same point in their run.

Ordering is the game

Everyone gets the same kind of cards. The only decision is sequence, and sequence changes everything: what you can afford, what you can survive, what compounds. Kill a monster before you visit the Boneyard and it pays. Buy a sword you can't afford yet and the slot fizzles — greyed out, wasted, path continues.

Combat is deterministic

No dice, no crits, ever. The same resolver runs path fights and the duel, so you learn the duel by playing the path. Every exchange, in order:

  1. Poison ticks on both sides (ignores Armour)
  2. First Strike lands — first exchange only
  3. Both sides attack at once: damage = ATK − Armour, minimum 1
  4. Thorns reflect back at whoever just hit you
  5. Rally adds its ATK for the rest of the fight

Because it's exact, breakpoints are real: against a 14 HP troll, going from 4 ATK to 5 saves you 5 HP — and going from 5 to 6 saves you nothing.

You can't die on the path

Path damage floors you at 1 HP. Arriving at the duel on 1 HP is punishment enough. Hearts — you have three — are only ever lost in the duel.

The run

Five duel wins completes a run. Three losses ends it. Between rounds you heal half your max HP; gold and ATK carry over in full.
